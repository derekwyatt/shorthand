# Manual Unit 22: omission of final t (source transcription)

Source: *Gregg Shorthand Manual, Anniversary Edition* (1929), Chapter VIII, Unit 22, printed
pages 97–99 (PDF pages 113–115 of the scan at https://greggshorthand.github.io/Annivman.pdf).
A 1929 US publication, in the US public domain since 1 January 2025.

Conventions are as in `manual-unit2.md`: tokens are stroke ids plus `a` / `e` / `i`
circles, the `o` / `u` hooks and the `h` dot, written in writing order; vowel marks are
omitted. The w of west is the oo hook running into a circle (`u e`, the Unit 11 w loop).
Plate crop boxes (80 dpi, printed page numbers) are in `scratchpad/u22/plates-unit22.json`,
ids `word-<word>`. Page 99 (§177) is reading and dictation practice.

## Rules, quoted from the manual

- **§174 Omission of final t.** "When slightly enunciated, t is omitted at the end of many
  words." Word drill headed "(t omitted after s)".
- **§175 Word drill.** "(t omitted after k, p, den)".
- **§176 Word drill.** "(t is written in the following words)".
- **§177 Reading and dictation practice**: connected text, not transcribed.

## Left out, and why

- **Same outline as an existing example:** test (`t e s-left`, word-tease), past
  (`p a s-left`, word-pass), act (`a k`, brief-accord); and in §176 cast, vast, least, taste
  and missed, which are already Unit 5 words (cast, vast, least, taste, mist) with the same
  outlines.
- **Too small or unclear on the plate:**
  - §174: earnest (the r and n after the circle can't be separated), request (the k and
    the final s run together), justice (the second s is not clear), and insist, consist,
    persist, resist, exist, exhaust (the final pair of s signs is one small curl; which
    signs, and whether a circle stands between them, can't be read).
  - §175: project and affect (the joins between pr / a and the following j / f are
    unclear), abrupt (the b–r–p run is compressed), president and student (the s and den
    blend run together).
  - §176: mixed (the k s after the circle is one small curl), worst (the w and r are not
    separable) and distant, intent, content, extent, patent (the -tent ending, ten with
    a written t, is too compressed to read the t).
- **Uncertain or not matching the plate:** modest (the reading with the o hook is
  uncertain) and disgust (the s–g–s joining doesn't come out as on the plate).
- **§177:** connected reading practice only.

## Inference (not stated by the manual)

- The s signs follow §49/§51: left s after p, b, r, l, t, d, n, m and o; right s after k,
  g, f, v, j and the oo hook, and with circles only (east, exact).
- con is k (Unit 9), in before d is the nd blend (induct, conduct), de before t or d is
  the ted blend (detest, detect, deduct), pro is pr (§127), and ex is es (§106).
- hardest and artist use the §71 left-motion circle for ar before the straight d / t, as
  the plates show a circle turned that way at the start.
- just, adjust, adjustment and disgust drop the short u before the s (§124 extended); the
  plates show no hook.
- largest drops r (§165), as large does in Unit 20.
