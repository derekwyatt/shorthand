# Manual Unit 30: phrasing principles (source transcription)

Source: *Gregg Shorthand Manual, Anniversary Edition* (1929), Chapter X, Unit 30, printed
pages 129–133 (PDF pages 145–149 of the scan at https://greggshorthand.github.io/Annivman.pdf).
A 1929 US publication, in the US public domain since 1 January 2025.

Conventions are as in `manual-unit29.md`: tokens are stroke ids plus `a` / `e` / `i`
circles, the `o` / `u` hooks, the `h` dot and the `w-dash`, written in writing order; vowel
marks are omitted. `^` ends a disjoined prefix: the tokens before it are written just above
the start of the rest. Plate crop boxes (80 dpi, printed page numbers) are in
`scratchpad/u30/plates-unit30.json`, ids `phrase-<phrase>`; they cover the Phrases table and
the "needing new marks" table. Page 132 (§225) is reading and dictation practice and page
133 (§226) writing practice.

## Rules, quoted from the manual

- **§218.** "The words misunderstand and misunderstood are expressed by stand and stood
  placed under mis, with mis placed on the line of writing. This rule is extended to the
  words understand and understood when they are preceded by a pronoun, a brief form, or a
  short phrase form:" misunderstand, misunderstood, I understood, we understood, I
  understand, I do not understand, I cannot understand, thoroughly understood.
- **§219.** "The words extra, enter, over, under, short, center, counter, agree, grand are
  expressed by the prefixal forms placed over the next word:" extra discount, enter the,
  enter into, over the, under any, under consideration, extra fare, short time, center
  line, agree with you.
- **§220.** "The word done is expressed by the den blend in many phrases:" have done, has
  been done, has done, will be done, would be done, should be done.
- **§221.** "In many phrases the word than is expressed by n." quicker than, better than,
  sooner than, rather than, nearer than, greater than.
- **§222.** "Many useful business phrases may be secured by slightly modifying the form for
  us:" give us, tell us, write us, to us, let us, mail us.
- **§223.** "In many phrases department is expressed by a disjoined d:" credit department,
  shipping department, purchasing department, accounting department.
- **§224.** "In a number of phrases the word forms are modified or a word is omitted where
  the grammatical construction of the sentence would compel its restoration when
  transcribing:" of course, at once, at any rate, great deal, I always, on hand, as
  follows, whole lot, one another, day's sight, do you know, great pleasure, your order,
  first class, whether or not, at all events, to some extent, to a great extent, to such
  an extent, at the same time, in other words, once in a while, in my opinion, in the
  first place, as soon as possible, as a matter of fact, on account of the fact, over and
  over again.
- **§225 Reading and dictation practice** and **§226 Writing practice**: connected text,
  not transcribed.

## Words needing new marks

Two placements the engine doesn't have. Proposed tokens:

- **`_` (§218, "placed under"):** the tokens after it are written as a separate stroke
  below the part before it. On the plates the second part (stand, stood) begins under the
  *end* of the first part, not under its start as with `^`: in misunderstand the stand
  sign's lowest, leftmost point sits below the tick of mis, about 0.3 of a line below the
  line of writing, and its long final curve rises to about the level of mis. After the
  large circle for I, stand/stood starts directly under the circle.
- **`|` (§223, disjoined d for department):** the tokens after it are written as a separate
  stroke on the line, just after the end of the part before it. On the plates the d is a
  full-length upward d that starts a little below the line, about 0.2 of a line to the
  right of the word's last point, and rises past the word's top, parallel to any d or t in
  the word.

## Left out, and why

- **§218 placed-under forms and §223 disjoined d:** not built yet (the `_` and `|` placements
  below), so every row of the "needing new marks" table is left out and has no plate crop.
- **§218 thoroughly understood:** the thoroughly sign can't be read without guessing.
- **§219 extra discount:** the discount rest is a tangled loop (discount was also left out
  of Unit 25); **agree with you:** built on the agr sign, which Unit 29 found no token
  reading for.
- **§220 has been done, has done:** the has form (a loop with a curl) can't be split into
  tokens with confidence.
- **§221 better than:** the end of the outline doesn't come out as on the plate.
- **§221 quicker than, rather than, greater than:** the forms before n (a looped k with the
  w dash; r with a loop; great) can't be read without guessing.
- **§222, all six:** the modified us is a looped curl, like a longhand ℓ, that no token
  draws.
- **§223 accounting department:** the account form before the d can't be read with
  confidence.
- **§224 in my opinion:** the end of the outline doesn't come out as on the plate.
- **§224 of course:** `o k`, the same outline as O.K. (Unit 25).
- **§224 do you know:** the first stroke is a curve on the plate where d u n o draws a
  straight d; the reading doesn't hold.
- **§224, the rest (23):** at once, at any rate, great deal, as follows, one another, day's
  sight, great pleasure, your order, first class, whether or not, at all events, to some
  extent, to a great extent, to such an extent, at the same time, in other words, once in
  a while, in the first place, as soon as possible, as a matter of fact, on account of the
  fact, over and over again. Modified and run-together forms that can't be split into
  tokens without guessing.
- **§225–226:** connected practice only.

## Inference (not stated by the manual)

- **§219** reuses the Unit 28–29 prefix signs: enter `n`, over `o`, under `u`, extra
  `e s-right`, short `sh`, center `s-right n`, with the next word under them as with `^`.
  into is the nd blend (nt), as the plate's one smooth rising curve; consideration reads
  `k s-right sh`, as the construction rest in Unit 28.
- **§220** done is the ten/den blend (`ten`); will be, would be, should be are the existing
  phrase forms (`l b`, `d b`, `sh d b`), have is `v`.
- **§221** than is `n` joined after the existing outlines: better (`b e t e-rev`), near
  (`n e-rev`) with the er circle repeated in nearer, and sooner as soon (`s-right n`)
  with the r circle; the e-rev circles stand between straight strokes as §71 requires.
- **§224** I always is the large I circle, a nearly level l and s (`i l s-left`); on hand
  and whole lot take the h dot over the o hook; in my opinion is n and m run into one long
  line, then the brief form opinion (`o p n`).

## Engine needs

- The proposed `_` placement (§218) and `|` placement (§223); the §218 I phrases also need
  a lone circle as the first part.
- Nothing else; every phrase in the Phrases table compiles.
