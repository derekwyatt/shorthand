# Manual Unit 15: omission of short u and ow, joined prefixes and suffixes (source transcription)

Source: *Gregg Shorthand Manual, Anniversary Edition* (1929), Chapter V, Unit 15, printed
pages 68–72 (PDF pages 84–88 of the scan at https://greggshorthand.github.io/Annivman.pdf).
A 1929 US publication, in the US public domain since 1 January 2025.

Conventions are as in `manual-unit2.md`: tokens are stroke ids plus `a` / `e` circles, the
`o` / `u` hooks and the `h` dot, written in writing order; vowel marks are omitted. Plate
crop boxes (80 dpi, printed page numbers) are in `scratchpad/u15/plates-unit15.json`, ids
`word-<word>` and `brief-<first word>`. Pages 71–72 (§131 reading, §132 writing practice)
are connected plates and text only.

## Rules, quoted from the manual

- **§124 Omission of short u and ow.** "In the body of a word short u and ow are omitted
  before n and m, and short u before straight downstrokes:"
- **§125.** "Between n-n, ow is indicated by a jog, as in announce, short u is inserted
  between n-n, n-m, as in nun and numb; moun is expressed by the men blend, as in
  mountain."
- **§126.** "The u is omitted in the termination sume:" assume, resume, consume, presume.
- **§127 Joined prefixes and suffixes.** "The syllables per, pro, pur are expressed by pr;
  the syllable ble, by b, ple, by p (in the words given below only); ment, by m:"
- **§128.** "When pro occurs before an upward character or k, it is more convenient to
  insert the vowel, as in:" protection, produce, produced.
- **§129 Compound joined prefixes.** "Two or more simple prefixes may be joined:"
  Footnote: "The initial vowel is not required in compound prefixes."
- **§130 Brief forms for common words.**

## Left out, and why

- **ton, done (§124):** the plates are the same outlines as town (`t n`) and down (`d n`).
- **brush (§124):** the plate overlaps judge, and the sh inside the br curve can't be read
  clearly.
- **announce (§124–125):** the ow jog between n-n has no token.
- **proper, process, perhaps, pursue, prove, perform, profit, valuable (§127):** the pr
  curve runs into the next character too tightly to tell the strokes apart.
- **sensible, miserable, suitable, available, reliable (§127):** the ble plates are tiny
  and overlap the b of the row above or below.
- **apartment, compliment, excitement (§127):** the junctions are not legible.
- **protection, produce, produced (§128):** the vowel inserted after pr and the endings
  are too compressed.
- **inform, conform (§129):** already examples, with these outlines, in Unit 6
  (`n f m`) and Unit 9 (`k f m`).
- **reconcile, recognize, unforeseen, unexplored, unimportant, unexpected,
  uncomfortable, unaccountable, uninformed, unemployed, unconscious (§129):** the plates
  have small ticks, disjoined endings or compressed junctions that can't be transcribed
  safely.
- **Brief forms problem, success, probable, except, correspond, organize, perfect /
  proof, satisfy, serious (§130):** the joinings are not clear enough on the plate.

## Changes to other levels

None.

## Engine needs

- **No new marks** for the 57 examples.
- **judge (`j j`):** two j strokes in one direction must read as one long line, as on the
  plate.
- **lumber, trouble, terrible, noble, payable (b at the end):** the b descends well below
  the line on the plates; check the joins into b.
- **sunshine:** the sh with an e circle at its foot before n; check the circle side.

## Inference (not stated by the manual)

- The s signs follow §49/§51: sun, sunk, summer, sunshine, simple, sample, stop take the
  right s (before n, nk, m, t); resume, presume and promise take the left s (after r, or
  after m); consume and assume take the right s (after k; with a circle only, before m).
- summer: the small final curl is read as e r.
- million: m, the e circle, l, then n (the o of the last syllable omitted before n).
- column: the small turn after k is read as the o hook.
- moment: m o m, the ment suffix written m (§127).
- example: es for ex, as §106 (Unit 12) gives, then ample.
- settlement: settle as in Unit 5 (`s-right e t l`) plus m; the e circle is not visible
  on this plate.
- incomplete: in + com + the ple of complete as p.
- person: pr then a left s (after r).
- excel: es for ex, then l.
