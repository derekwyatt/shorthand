# Manual Unit 28: analogical word beginnings, disjoined (source transcription)

Source: *Gregg Shorthand Manual, Anniversary Edition* (1929), Chapter X, Unit 28, printed
pages 121–124 (PDF pages 137–140 of the scan at https://greggshorthand.github.io/Annivman.pdf).
A 1929 US publication, in the US public domain since 1 January 2025.

Conventions are as in `manual-unit2.md`: tokens are stroke ids plus `a` / `e` / `i`
circles, the `o` / `u` hooks, the `h` dot and the `w-dash`, written in writing order; vowel
marks are omitted. `^` ends a §209 disjoined prefix: the tokens before it are the prefix,
written just above the start of the rest. Plate crop boxes (80 dpi, printed page numbers) are in
`scratchpad/u28/plates-unit28.json`, ids `word-<word>`. The foot of page 123 and page 124
(§213) are reading and dictation practice.

## Rules, quoted from the manual

- **§209 Analogical word-beginnings, disjoined.** "Certain prefixes or letters are
  disjoined to express tr and a following vowel. The prefix is placed above the line, very
  close to the remainder of the word:" The rows are centr-, center; contr-, counter;
  constr-; detr-, deter; distr-, destr-; electr- (or electric); extr-, exter (or excl-);
  intr-, inter, enter (or intel); instr-; retr-; restr-.
- **§210 Key to analogical word-beginnings.**
  1. central, center, centralize, centralization, centrifugal.
  2. contract, contrary, control, contribute, contrast, counterpart, countersign.
  3. construct, construction, constrain, constraint, construe, construed.
  4. detriment, detrimental, deteriorate, detract, detraction.
  5. destroy, distribute, distribution, distract, distraction.
  6. electric, electrical, electrolysis, electric light.
  7. extra, extreme, extraordinary, exterior, extricate, exclusive, exclamation.
  8. interest, interesting, enter, entered, entertain, interfere, introduce, intelligence.
  9. instruct, instruction, instrument, instruments, instrumental.
  10. retreat, retract, retraction, retribution, retrieve, retrogression.
  11. restrain, restraint, restrict, restriction.
- **§211.** "In forming the derivatives of words ending in ct, as in contract, it is not
  necessary to disjoin to express ed, or, er, or ive. The t is omitted in the primitive
  form (under the rules given in Chapter VIII), and also in its derivatives:" contracted,
  contractor, constructed, constructor, constructive, instructed, instructor, instructive,
  extracted, restrictive, detracted, affected, active, defective, effected, detected,
  effective, detective.
- **§212 Analogical word-beginnings, compounds.** "Some very useful forms are obtained by
  joining simple syllable characters, such as in, un, dis, re, non, to the signs for
  disjoined word-beginnings:" uncontrolled, unrestrained, uninteresting, uninstructed,
  concentration, reconstruction, redistribute, disinterested, indestructible, inextricable,
  eccentric, misinterpret.
- **§213 Reading and dictation practice**: connected text, not transcribed.

## Left out, and why

- **§209, can't read the rest without guessing:** centralize, centralization (the l ends in
  a loop crossed by a tail that can't be split into tokens); detrimental, instrumental (the
  m runs into a dipping curve that may be l or the nt blend); deteriorate (a small loop and
  curve); destroy (a small looped mark under the prefix); electrolysis; extraordinary,
  exterior, extricate, exclusive (tangled rests, exclusive apparently joined to the
  prefix); interfere (f, a loop and a line: the loop's vowel and the line's letter are
  unclear); retrogression.
- **§209, needs a mark the app doesn't have:** interesting (the ing dot).
- **§209, a phrase:** electric light: the prefix over l and a loop, with no k; left out as
  a phrase whose reading of light is a guess.
- **§212:** uninteresting (ing dot); uninstructed (un and in run together as one long
  line, which the engine can't draw as n n); concentration, eccentric, misinterpret,
  disinterested, inextricable, indestructible (the compound prefix signs can't be split
  into tokens with confidence; indestructible's rest also tangles with inextricable on the
  plate).
- **§209, enter:** alone it is a single n, the same outline as the brief form for in/not.
- **§213:** connected reading practice only.
- No word in the unit has numerals.

## Inference (not stated by the manual)

- **§209 rows matched to the §210 key by position.** Every row has exactly as many outlines
  as its key line has words (5, 7, 6, 5, 5, 4, 7, 8, 5, 6, 4), so the match is one to one.
  "...." stands where the prefix alone is the word: center, construe, extra, enter.
- **The s in the prefix signs** follows the app's existing joinings: `k s-right` as in
  consume and accede, `n s-left` as in dance, `d s-left` as in needs, `r s-left` as in
  curse, `e s-right` as in eastern and example. The centr sign's s turns like the top of a
  2, so `s-right n`. Each was checked against an engine rendering of the other direction.
- **Rests:** act is `k` and action `k sh`, as in action (`a k sh`); the t of ct is omitted
  (§211). ary is `r e`, tion/sion `sh`, ment `m`, ive `v`, bute `b`,
  ment-s `m s-left`, ain `n`, aint `nd`, eed `d`, ain in entertain `ten`, as in certain.
- **§211:** or/er after k is `r` (contractor, constructor, instructor); ed is the joined
  short tick `t` (contracted, constructed, instructed, extracted, detracted). "It is not
  necessary to disjoin to express ed", so it is not the §182 disjoined t.
- **§212:** the joined syllables go before `^` with the prefix: un `n`, re `r`; uncontrolled
  ends in the ld blend; reconstruction is `r k s-right`, redistribute `r d s-left`.
- **The seven joined §211 words** (affected, active, defective, effected, detected,
  effective, detective) are read from their primitives already in the app: effect `f k`
  (Unit 12), defect `def e k` and detect `ted e k` (Unit 22). affected shows the a circle at
  the head of f and no circle between f and k; effected and effective show no initial
  circle, matching effect `f k`.
- **Vowels not shown are not written:** contrast (`k ^ s-right`, no a circle visible),
  counterpart (`k ^ p t`), centrifugal (`s-right n ^ f`, one short curve for -ifugal).

## Engine needs

- None new. The §209 prefix uses `^` (one per outline, not first or last), which the
  engine now supports. The prefix-alone words (center, construe, extra, enter) have no `^`.
- enter is a lone `n`, the same outline as the brief form in/not; a read quiz could show
  two identical outlines.
