# Manual Unit 29: analogical word beginnings, disjoined, continued (source transcription)

Source: *Gregg Shorthand Manual, Anniversary Edition* (1929), Chapter X, Unit 29, printed
pages 125–128 (PDF pages 141–144 of the scan at https://greggshorthand.github.io/Annivman.pdf).
A 1929 US publication, in the US public domain since 1 January 2025.

Conventions are as in `manual-unit28.md`: tokens are stroke ids plus `a` / `e` / `i`
circles, the `o` / `u` hooks, the `h` dot and the `w-dash`, written in writing order; vowel
marks are omitted. `^` ends a disjoined prefix: the tokens before it are the prefix, written
just above the start of the rest. Plate crop boxes (80 dpi, printed page numbers) are in
`scratchpad/u29/plates-unit29.json`, ids `word-<word>`. The foot of page 127 and page 128
(§217) are reading and dictation practice.

## Rules, quoted from the manual

- **§214 Analogical word-beginnings, disjoined (continued).** The rows are agr-, aggr-;
  ant-; decl-; incl-; magn- (or Mc); multi; over; para*; post*; recl-; self, circu, circum;
  grand; short, ship; super, supre; susp-, suscep; trans; under. The footnote: "The prefix
  para is written above the rest of the word; post is written on the line close before the
  following character."
- **§215 Key to analogical word-beginnings.**
  1. agree, agreeable, agreement, agriculture, aggravate, aggressive, disagree, disagreeable.
  2. anticipate, anticipation, antagonize, antecedent, anterior.
  3. declare, declaration, decline, declined, declaim, declamation.
  4. include, incline, inclination, inclined, inclusion, inclusive, inclement.
  5. magnify, magnitude, magnificent, magnet, McNeil.
  6. multitude, multiple, multiply, multiplication.
  7. over, overlook, overtake, overcoat, overthrow, overcome.
  8. paragraph, parallel, paramount, paradise, paralysis, parasite.
  9. postage, postal, postmaster, postpone, postman.
  10. recline, reclined, reclaim, reclamation, recluse.
  11. selfish, self-confident, self-control, circular, circulation, circumstances, circus.
  12. grand, grandson, granddaughter, grandmother, grandfather.
  13. short, shorter, shorten, shortage, shortly, shortsighted, shipshape, shipwreck,
      shipyard.
  14. superintend, superior, supervise, support, supreme, supremacy, superb, supersede.
  15. suspect, suspected, suspicious, suspicion, susceptible, suspend, suspense, suspension.
  16. transact, transacted, transaction, transfer, translation, transport, transfix.
  17. under, understanding, undertake, understood, underneath, underline, underwrite.
- **§216 Analogical word-beginnings, compounds (continued).** No rule text; the words are
  self-interest, unselfish, unparalleled, unsuspected, self-control, unsuspicious,
  disinclined, disinclination, self-contradiction, unsusceptible, untransacted,
  unrestricted.
- **§217 Reading and dictation practice**: connected text, not transcribed.

## Left out, and why

- **agr-, aggr- (8 words):** the sign is a narrow slanting loop joined to the rest, which no
  token or pair of tokens draws; it does not read as an a circle with g.
- **ant- (5 words):** a large circle alone above the rest (as incl), and the rests
  (anticipate, antagonize, antecedent, anterior) can't be read without guessing.
- **decl-, incl-:** inclusion, inclusive and inclement have rests that can't be read with
  confidence (a short slash, a long curve with a curl, a line turned up).
- **magn-:** magnitude and magnificent (rests unclear; magnificent's curve is close to
  magnify's); McNeil (a proper name with the capital mark).
- **multi (4 words):** the sign, a level line turned down at its end, doesn't read as any
  token pair with confidence.
- **over:** over itself (the o hook alone, the same outline as the brief form of); overlook
  and overthrow (the rests' last strokes are unclear).
- **para:** paramount, paradise, paralysis, parasite (looped rests that can't be split into
  tokens).
- **post (5 words):** written on the line before the rest, not above it; needs the proposed
  `>` (see the table above). postmaster and postpone are also unclear.
- **self, circu, circum:** self-confident, self-control, circular, circulation,
  circumstances, circus (the circu sign and the rests run together and can't be read
  without guessing).
- **grand:** grand itself (the g alone, the same outline as the brief form go/good);
  granddaughter and grandfather (looped rests that don't match an engine reading).
- **short, ship:** short itself (sh alone); shortage (two slashes of nearly equal length:
  sign and j can't be told apart); shortly (needs a lone-circle rest); shortsighted,
  shipshape, shipwreck, shipyard (rests unclear).
- **super, supre:** superintend, superior, supervise, supremacy, supersede (rests unclear).
- **susp-, suscep (8 words):** the sign is an S-shaped curve that no token pair reads with
  confidence.
- **trans:** transfer, transport, transfix (rests unclear).
- **under:** under itself; understanding (ing dot); understood, underneath, underline,
  underwrite (rests unclear).
- **§216:** self-interest, unselfish, unparalleled, unsuspected, self-control,
  unsuspicious, disinclined, disinclination, self-contradiction, unsusceptible (compound
  signs that can't be split into tokens, or built on the left-out incl and susp signs);
  untransacted (the un-t sign is a hooked curve that doesn't read as `n t`).
- **§217:** connected reading practice only.

None.

## Inference (not stated by the manual)

- **Rows matched to the §215 key by position.** Each row has as many outlines as its key
  line has words; "...." stands where the prefix alone is the word (agree, over, grand,
  short, under).
- **Rests** follow Unit 28's readings: ine `n`, ined `nd`, aim `m`, amation `m sh`, act
  `a k` (here the a circle is plain on the plate), acted `a k t`, action `a k sh`, er `r`,
  en `n`, port `t`, eme `m`, take `t a k`, coat `k o t`, come `k m`, graph `g`, allel `l`,
  son `s-right n` (as center's sign in Unit 28), mother `m th-over` (as mother, Unit 20),
  lation `l a sh`, use `s-right`.
- **The signs' directions** follow the app's existing strokes: the super sign curves like f
  (`s-right`), the self sign like p (`s-left`); the over sign is the o hook and the under
  sign the oo hook, written above.
- **unrestricted** is un + restr (`n r s-left`, as unrestrained in Unit 28) over ict `k t`.

## Engine needs

- A lone circle as a disjoined prefix (incl) or as the whole rest (shortly).
- The proposed `>` for post: a prefix written on the line, close before the rest.
- Nothing else; every word in the Words table compiles with `^`.
